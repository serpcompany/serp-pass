# Keep the subscription on the platform and settle Publishers separately

Status: **accepted for the private pilot**

SERP Apps Pass will own the Subscriber's Stripe subscription and later settle Publisher Earnings through Stripe Connect separate charges and transfers. One bundle payment can benefit multiple Publishers and the allocation may not be known when the charge occurs, so destination or direct charges tied to one connected account cannot represent the product; Apps Pass therefore owns the earnings ledger and Stripe executes only the resulting Transfers.
